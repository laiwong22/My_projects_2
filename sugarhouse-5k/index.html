Start & finish

Start and finish are at the same spot, by the pavilion north of the pond. Run west about 0.18 mi on the path to the loop at the pond's northwest corner and turn left (south). Run two counterclockwise laps, then turn right at the same corner and run back to the finish.

The hills

Each lap has two climbs: a gradual pull from the pond up to the high south side, and the big one, from the Parleys Creek crossing on the east side up to the north end of the park. The north side then rolls back down to the pond. The last 0.18 mi back to the finish is a gentle uphill.

About this map

The route is traced from the course map you planned and scaled to 5.00 km. Elevations are modeled on the park's terrain, using its published figure of about 92 ft of climbing per loop. They're close but not survey-grade, so check the course with a GPS watch before race day.
