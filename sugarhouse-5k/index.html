Start & finish

Start on the north side of the loop near the 2100 South entrance, running counterclockwise (heading west first). Lap 2 begins when you pass the start arch. After lap 2, carry on 0.35 mi (558 m) to the finish on the northwest side.

The hills

Each lap has two climbs: a long pull from the pond up around the southwest corner to the high ground along the south side, and a shorter rise up the east side after you drop down to Parleys Creek. The south side runs high and rolling.

About this map

The loop is the park's inner paved road, 1.38 mi with about 92 ft of climbing per lap. The route line and elevation curve are drawn to those figures and are close but not survey-grade. Check the course with a GPS watch before race day.
