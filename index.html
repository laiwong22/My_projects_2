Controls

  • W / S or ↑ / ↓ — move paddle
  • Mouse or touch — drag the paddle
  • Move the paddle as you hit to add spin — the ball swerves and kicks off the bounce
  • Tab / Space, a mouse click or the SMASH button — smash
  • The ball speeds up with every hit from the 4th hit of a rally
  • Let it bounce twice on your side and you lose the point
  • A shot whose first bounce lands past a sideline is out
  • Esc / P — pause
  • M — toggle sound
