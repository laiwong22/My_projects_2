PICKLE PONG

Pong on a pickleball court — every shot must bounce once.

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag on the court. Serve: Space or tap (hold ↑/↓ to aim). Pause: Esc · Mute: M
Rules: the ball must bounce exactly once on your side before you hit it. Hitting it in the air is a volley fault, and if it bounces twice you lose the point. Serves go diagonally and must land past the kitchen.
Physics: where the ball hits your paddle sets the angle. Move toward the net as you hit for a harder, flatter shot with more topspin. It might go long. Moving sideways adds spin that curves the ball.
